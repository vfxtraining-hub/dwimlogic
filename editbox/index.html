When the hours run out

Keep everything, or start clean.

Simple storage rules, written down before you pay.

Archive subscription

One subscription keeps it all

For $19 a month per project, the project files, every render and the final deliverables stay stored for as long as the subscription is active. Cancel any time.

Source footage

Cleared after 30 days unused

Your original footage is removed once it has not been used for 30 days, with or without an archive. You get a reminder first, and you still have your own copy.

No subscription

Files go, the record stays

If the hours are used and you decline the archive, project files are removed after a short download window. The message log and hour ledger stay, and you can start a new project any time.

Need more work

Top up and carry on

Buy another pack of hours and the project picks up where it left off, with every version and note still in place.

Questions

Before you send footage.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. You describe the job, we reply with a range of hours and the smallest pack that starts it. Nothing is charged until you choose a pack.

What happens if the job needs more hours than I bought?

The editor stops before the balance runs out and tells you in the project thread. You top up, or you take what has been delivered so far.

How big can my files be?

Large uploads go straight to storage. For very big shoots, paste a transfer link from WeTransfer, Frame.io, Dropbox or Google Drive instead.

Who can see my project?

You, and the DWIM Logic team working on it. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email, and each project is visible only to the client who opened it.

What do I get at the end?

Finished renders to the spec you set (resolution, frame rate, captions). If you need to keep editing, ask for the DaVinci Resolve project or an XML timeline.
